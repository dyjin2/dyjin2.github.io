About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University, advised by Prof. Yu Jiang in CAIR. My research focuses on embodied and agentic AI systems that integrate reasoning and action for scientific discovery. I work on autonomous scientific workflows, robotic laboratory automation, and intelligent systems that interact with the physical world.

Previously, I received my B.E. in Agriculture Engineering from Zhejiang University, where I worked on magnetic microrobots for single-cell manipulation.
